# Scaling Up or Shutting Out? Firm Size and Female Employment in India

## Overview
This project studies the relationship between firm size and female employment in India’s manufacturing sector using firm-level data from the Annual Survey of Industries (ASI) 2023–24.

The project examines whether larger firms employ a higher share of blue-collar women workers compared to smaller firms.

## Research Question
Does firm size impact the share of blue-collar women workers in the Indian manufacturing sector?

## Motivation
Female labour force participation in India remains low, especially in manufacturing industries. Existing literature suggests that larger firms may provide better workplace amenities, safety measures, maternity benefits, and employment opportunities that encourage higher female participation.

This project explores whether firm size is positively associated with the employment share of women workers.

## Dataset
- Source: Annual Survey of Industries (ASI) 2023–24
- Type: Firm-level manufacturing data
- Observations: ~48,900 factories

## Variables

### Dependent Variable
Share of directly hired blue-collar women workers:

Female Blue-Collar Workers / Total Blue-Collar Workers

### Independent Variable
Firm size measured using:
- Total employees in the factory
- Log of total employees

## Methodology

- Descriptive statistics
- Exploratory data analysis

## Key Preliminary Findings
- Women account for approximately 12% of workers in manufacturing factories.
- Larger firms appear to employ a relatively higher share of women workers.
- Positive correlation observed between total employees and female employment share.
